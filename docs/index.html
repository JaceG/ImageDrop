Any format to any format

Drop a HEIC from your iPhone and get a JPEG. Turn a PNG into a WebP for the web. The converted copy is saved right next to the original.

🎚️

Presets, not sliders

Pick where the image is going — web page, social media, email, thumbnail — and ImageDrop chooses sensible quality and size limits for you.

Private by design

Everything happens on your Mac using Apple's built-in image engine. Nothing is uploaded, no account, no tracking.

Batch friendly

Drop a whole selection of files at once, or use “Open With” from Finder. Photos are auto-rotated the right way up.

Install in a minute

ImageDrop is a free, independently made app, so macOS asks you to confirm the first launch.

  1. Download the zip above, unzip it, and drag ImageDrop into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it once. macOS will say it can't verify the developer. That's expected — the app isn't notarized with Apple (which costs a yearly developer fee).
  3. Allow it. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. Or, from Terminal: 
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/ImageDrop.app
  4. Look up. A photo icon appears in your menu bar. Click it to open the drop panel.

WebP export uses the free cwebp tool from Homebrew (brew install webp). Every other format works out of the box.
